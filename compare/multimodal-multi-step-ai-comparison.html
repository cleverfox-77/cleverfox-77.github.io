1. Verified Competitor Limitations on Multimodal Inputs & Complex Multi-Step Tasks

Intercom Fin (fin.ai) — Verified Boundaries

  • Non-Linear Multi-Step Drift: Fin Procedures rely on predictable user paths; when a customer switches topics mid-procedure (e.g., asking a billing question halfway through an exchange flow), Fin can lose its place in the procedure stack or escalate prematurely.
  • Rigid API Failure Recovery: If an external webhook or connector returns an unexpected error mid-procedure, Fin cannot dynamically troubleshoot and defaults to human handover.
  • Mandatory Voice Latency Filler: Fin Voice injects system-level acknowledgement phrases ("Let me look into that for you") to mask lookup latency that cannot be suppressed via standard guidance.
  • Outcome Meter Ambiguity: Bills $0.99 per outcome (50/mo minimum) where the system itself defines what counts as resolved.

respond.io — Verified Boundaries

  • 3-Minute Inbound Voice Cap & No Outbound Calls: respond.io's AI voice skill is restricted to inbound calls only, hard-capped at 3 minutes per call to prevent drift, and gated behind higher-tier plans.
  • Manual Flowchart Offloading for Multi-Step Logic: For complex branching, calculations, or multi-step database queries, respond.io advises offloading logic to manual Workflows rather than autonomous multi-step planning.
  • 20-Message Context Window Boundaries: Ad context blocks and deep historical metadata have strict message-window cutoffs.
  • Punitive MAC Contact Scaling: Charges base tier fees plus $12–$15 per 100 extra Monthly Active Contacts whenever marketing campaigns spike lead volume.

Gorgias AI Agent — Verified Boundaries

  • No Native Voice Support (Text/Written Channels Only): Gorgias AI Agent operates strictly on written digital channels (chat, email, SMS, social DMs) and cannot answer or place phone calls natively.
  • E-Commerce Only Lock-In: Built exclusively around retail storefronts (Shopify, BigCommerce, Magento); cannot serve SaaS, healthcare, clinics, salons, fintech, or B2B service workflows.
  • Multi-Intent Escalation Ceiling: When shoppers stack multiple demands in one message or hit policy gray areas, Gorgias defaults to human handover, capping automation around 30%–50%.
  • Double Meter Billing: Charges helpdesk ticket overages ($0.36–$0.40) plus $1.50 per AI automated interaction past plan allowances.

Tidio (Lyro AI) & Ada CX — Verified Boundaries

  • Tidio Lyro is 100% Text-Only: Lyro does not support native voice calls, cannot transcribe WhatsApp/Instagram audio notes, and cannot visually inspect customer-uploaded product photos.
  • Tidio Circuit-Board Flowchart Loops: Off-script or compound customer messages cause Tidio Flows to loop endlessly without reaching a human, while Lyro credits burn on basic greetings.
  • Ada CX Legacy Playbook Rigidity & $30k–$300k/yr Contracts: Ada requires 8–16 weeks of onboarding, offers zero self-serve trial, and layers LLM reasoning over legacy 2016-era Playbook trees.
