    2. Deep Dive: The 7 Deterministic Steps Inside OctaDezx

    Every claim on octadezx.com/enterprise-multi-step-inquiries and octadezx.com/benchmarks/multi-step-inquiries maps directly to deterministic production code:

    1. 01 — Detect (Rule-Based Multilingual Compound Detection): Identifies whether a message contains one request or several across English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Dutch, Polish, plus code-switched Spanglish, Portuñol, Franglais, and Banglish.
    2. 02 — Plan (Dependency-Aware Step DAG): Builds an ordered execution plan with explicit step dependencies (for example, an item size exchange waits on the order lookup it belongs to). Works on both written chat and live phone calls.
    3. 03 — Gate (Reference & Ownership Gates): Verifies that an order or appointment booking belongs to the authenticated customer asking before any record details enter the model's context. A refused reference is indistinguishable from a missing one (0 leaks in 24 holdout privacy tests).
    4. 04 — Lookup (Deterministic Records & Code Math): Reads orders, bookings, stock, prices, and policies directly from structured records. All currency, refund, and date/time arithmetic is computed in deterministic code — never delegated to the LLM.
    5. 05 — Clarify or Trace-Attached Handoff: When a required fact is missing or ambiguous, OctaDezx asks a specific clarifying question or escalates to your human inbox with the full step plan and audit trace attached so the customer never repeats themselves.
    6. 06 — Verify (Deterministic Final Verifier): Checks every written reply against the live knowledge graph before transmission, refusing any dollar figure, percentage, time window, discount code, or promise not backed by verified records (refused 59 of 61 injected faults on holdout).
    7. 07 — Action (Pre-Commit Action Guard): Blocks any order placement, refund filing, or appointment booking that the customer did not explicitly confirm (6 of 6 unrequested actions blocked).
